### About Us

My name is Saif Aziz, a father of three from Gaza City, one of these 3 families.

Since October 2023, We have been forcibly displaced more than 30 times. Our homes in Al-Shujaiya were destroyed, and we now live in makeshift tents - 3 displaced families from the same neighborhood (12 people: my family of 5, a neighbor family of 4, and a neighbor family of 3, including 5 children).

We share everything equally.

### How Donations Are Used

- $25 = Bread and water for one day for our community
- $50 = Vegetables for one day for our community
- $100 = Bread, water and vegetables for 3 days for all families
- $40 = Milk for small children
- $350 = Full food parcel for a week for 3 families

### Why Crypto? Banks Are Blocked

PayPal and banks are blocked in Gaza. Giveth is our only direct way.

Since the war started, life has been extremely difficult. We have no income, no jobs, and everything is expensive. We spend most of our day trying to find water and bread. The tents do not protect us from heat or cold. The children are sick because of the bad water and lack of food. We are trying to stay strong and support each other.

Every donation you make will be divided equally between the three families. It will go directly to buying food, water, and milk for the children. This is the only way we can keep our families alive right now. We have no other support.
