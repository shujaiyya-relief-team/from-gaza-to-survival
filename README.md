### About Us

My name is Saif Aziz, a father of three from Gaza City. This project supports 3 families (12 people total) displaced from Al-Shujaiya after our homes were destroyed. We live in tents and share everything as one community kitchen.

### Why Crypto? Banks Are Blocked

In Gaza, all banks, PayPal, and international money transfers are completely blocked and banned. We cannot receive money by any normal way. This is why we chose crypto donations through Giveth. It is the ONLY way our community can receive direct support from the outside world without a bank.

### How Donations Are Used (100% Transparent)

- $25 = Bread and water for one day
- $100 = Bread, water, vegetables for 3 days for 3 families
- $350 = Full food parcel for a week for 3 families
- $40 = Milk for children

100% shared equally between the 3 families.
